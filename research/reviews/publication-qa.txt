Publication quality assurance
Project: Fuel tankering and East Asian aviation security
Publication date: 2026-11-12
Method: Python renderer checks, PDF text inspection and raster inspection.
No browser was run for these publication checks. This record does not claim a
browser, screen-reader, Microsoft Word or LibreOffice visual test.

Renderer and development checks
- The renderer creates the report PDF and standalone reflection PDF/DOCX from
  research/report.json, without fetching content from the network.
- Temporary development fixtures were stored outside the repository and were
  never used as the project source or distributed final documents.
- Repeated fixture builds produced identical PDF and DOCX SHA-256 hashes.
- PDF and DOCX metadata use the publication date in the source JSON.
- PDF document language is en-US. Section bookmarks are present. The DOCX uses
  semantic Title and Heading styles. The PDFs are not claimed to be PDF/UA or
  fully tagged accessible documents.
- The renderer rejects reflections outside 800-1,000 words in ordinary builds,
  rejects a declared count that differs from its count, and checks that numeric
  citations in report narrative resolve to declared reference IDs.
- Counting excludes citation-only markers; hyphenated compounds count as one;
  en/em dashes separate words. Only reflection paragraphs are counted.
