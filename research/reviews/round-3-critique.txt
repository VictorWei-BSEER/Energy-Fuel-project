WHAT WAS CHECKED AND HOLDS UP
1. Denominators: one tonne useful fuel delivered requires 1.16279 tonnes extra origin uplift at four hours; the separate one-tonne gross-origin scenario delivers 0.86 tonnes. The corresponding 0.51442 versus 0.4424 tonnes CO2 are not interchangeable. Report and reflection distinguish them correctly.
2. Counterfactuals: normal price comparison explicitly requires both purchase options to be available. The outage case reports physical destination uplift displaced, not cancellation reduction or the value of keeping a flight operating.
3. Sensitivity: 486 route-grid cases and 36 central cases match the package design. Finite minima/maxima are not presented as confidence intervals. The exponential rule is a structural comparison, not an empirical validation of the linear rule.
4. Geometry: route distances are observed-coordinate-derived; speed/routing/time are scenarios. No scheduled flight duration or aircraft-specific fuel performance is claimed.
5. Financial evidence: USD 1,000/t and USD 100/200 per tonne CO2 are explicitly illustrative. No absent airport quote is imputed. The zero-fee result is described as a lower-cost model case, not evidence of free handling or absent payload costs.
6. Asian security: the report separates crude/refinery exposure, national stocks, airport delivery systems and airline access. EIA's 89% figure is for crude and condensate through Hormuz to Asia in the first half of 2025, not all jet-fuel supply. No outage probability is inferred from that figure.
7. Institutional inference: Hong Kong installed storage is not equated with usable stocks/days of cover; Singapore's announced SAF coordination is not proof of present fuel adequacy; Japan's March 2026 national stock-release commitment is not a November Narita jet-fuel inventory.
8. Policy scope: the European 90% obligation is identified as annual, by operator and Union airport, rather than per-flight or applicable in Asia. National allocation, contract rights, competing demands and emissions trade-offs are acknowledged.
9. Research honesty: the report does not claim causal identification, a measured operational optimum or a production trading system. The proposed empirical validation stage specifies the missing evidence and comparison against actual uplift decisions.
