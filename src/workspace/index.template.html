Methods & scope ＋

Account for the fuel burned to carry fuel.

Minimize purchased fuel cost on a fixed rotation. Arrival fuel + uplift − taxi burn = take-off fuel. Extra fuel carried increases flight burn at the stated per-leg rate. Each leg respects tank, take-off mass, landing mass, reserve and uplift limits.

The local-uplift baseline uses the same initial and terminal fuel. When a supply cap makes that baseline infeasible, the app reports a feasibility difference rather than a savings percentage.

Model boundary

Two to five fixed legs; mass in metric tonnes and prices in USD/t. Quotations must include applicable into-plane fees and match the planning date. Convert other currencies and volume units before import.

Linear carriage coefficients and illustrative burn do not replace flight-performance calculations. This version does not optimize route choice, forecast prices or execute fuel orders.
